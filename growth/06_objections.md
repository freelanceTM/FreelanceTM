# Objection handling — memorize these

**"Dorogo / expensive"**
→ "Odno video, kotoroe privedyot 2 klientov, uzhe okupit nedelyu. Davayte sdelaem probnyy
za 30 AZN — esli ne zaydyot, vy nichem ne riskuete."

**"U menya net vremeni snimat'"**
→ "Vam nuzhno tol'ko snyat' 3-4 koroktikh kadra na telefon. Ya delayu vsyo ostal'noe.
5 minut vashego vremeni v nedelyu."

**"A pochemu ya doljen vam doverat'?"**
→ "Davayte bez predoplaty za pervyy: ya delayu probnyy Reels, vy platite tol'ko esli
ponravilos'. Riska nol'." (use this to remove friction on deal #1–5)

**"Mне eto ne nuzhno / у меня и так норм"**
→ "Ponyal. Mogu prosto skinut' 1 primer, chto by ya sdelal dlya vashego biznesa?
Besplatno, bez obyazatel'stv." (foot-in-the-door → many convert after seeing it)

**"Podumayu"**
→ "Konechno. Davayte ya zabroniruyu vam mesto na etu nedelyu i skinu probnik segodnya —
posmotrite gotovyy rezul'tat, potom reshite." (move to action, not 'maybe')

**Editor: "A ti zaplatish'?"**
→ "Oplata srazu posle sdachi raboty, do etogo klient uzhe oplatil mne. Pervyy zakaz
nebol'shoy — proverim drug druga."

## Golden rule
Always end EVERY message with a concrete next step (a question or an action),
never with a statement. Statements get ignored. Questions get replies.
