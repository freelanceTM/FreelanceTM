# Supply first — recruit 15–20 editors THIS WEEK

> Marketplaces die from empty supply. Get editors BEFORE clients so you can deliver
> instantly when a client says yes. You onboard them by hand.

## Where to find Reels/TikTok editors
- Telegram chats: freelance / SMM / video editing / student groups
- Instagram: search #montajturkmen / #reelsediting / local SMM hashtags, check who edits
- Universities / design courses (hungry juniors, low price, high effort)
- Ask existing editors "who else is good?" (referral chain)

## Qualify in 60 seconds (ask for)
- 2–3 portfolio samples (judge quality)
- Turnaround time (must do 1–2 days)
- Price per Reel (your margin lives here)
- Reliability signal (do they reply fast? if not → skip)

## Onboarding (you do it manually)
1. Create their gig profile yourself (title, 3 samples, price, delivery time).
2. Set expectations: deadlines are sacred. One missed deadline = removed.
3. Get their preferred payout method.
4. Add to a private "Editors" Telegram group for fast dispatch.

## Goal
- [ ] 15–20 editors qualified
- [ ] 5–8 of them "fast & reliable" tier (your A-team for first deals)
- [ ] 3 gig "showcases" published (your marketing material)
