# Demand — get clients (cold outreach that converts)

> Don't sell "a platform". Sell a RESULT: more views, more customers, less hassle.
> Target: small businesses that NEED video but have no time/skill to make it.

## Who to target (warm-to-cold order)
1. Businesses already posting BAD/inconsistent Reels (they get the value, need help).
2. Businesses with ZERO video but active on Instagram (cafes, salons, shops, clinics, gyms).
3. Local brands / online stores running promos.

## Channels
- Instagram DM (highest intent — you see their content)
- Telegram business chats / local entrepreneur groups
- Walk into 5 local cafes/shops and pitch in person (insanely effective, nobody does it)

## Cold DM #1 — Instagram (business with weak video)
---
Privet! Smotryu vash profil — produkt klassnyy, no Reels mozhno vyvesti na drugoy uroven'
(seychas oni teryayutsya v lente).

Ya delayu montazh Reels pod biznes: trendovyy montazh, subtitry, hook v pervye 3 sek.
Pervyy Reels — probno, deshevo, chtoby vy uvideli raznitsu.

Skinut' 2 primera i tsenu? Zaymyot u vas 1 minutu.
---

## Cold DM #2 — outcome-led (no time)
---
Privet! Delayu Reels/TikTok dlya biznesa pod klyuch — vy prosto prisylaete video s
telefona, ya vozvrashchayu gotovyy roliki dlya postinga.

3-5 Reels v nedelyu, vy nichego ne montiruете sami. Hotite tsenu na nedelyu?
---

## In-person pitch (cafe/shop owner) — 15 seconds
"Zdravstvuyte! Ya delayu korotkie video dlya biznesa — Reels, kotorye privodyat klientov.
Mogu sdelat' odno probnoe video vashego zavedeniya nedorogo, chtoby vy uvideli rezul'tat.
Davayte ya voz'mu paru kadrov pryamo seychas?"

## The OFFER that closes (use this)
- **Probnyy Reels — 30-50 AZN** (loss-leader to remove risk)
- **Nedelnyy paket: 4 Reels — 250 AZN** (your real money + retention)
- **Garantiya:** ne ponravilos' — peredelaem besplatno 1 raz.

## Volume target
- [ ] 30 personal DMs/day
- [ ] 5 in-person pitches/day (if possible)
- [ ] Track replies in `05_deal_tracker.csv`

## Conversion math
30 DMs → ~5–8 replies → ~1–2 trial orders. To get 5 deals/week you need ~150 DMs.
Selling is a numbers game. Send more.
