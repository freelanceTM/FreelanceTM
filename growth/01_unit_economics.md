# Unit Economics — know your numbers cold

> If you can't recite these, you're not running a business, you're running a hobby.

## Inputs (edit these to YOUR reality)
- Avg order value (AOV): **70 AZN**
- Commission: **10%** → **7 AZN revenue per order**
- Payment processing cost (~3% if card): **~2 AZN/order** → net ~5 AZN/order
- Your time per concierge deal (early): ~30 min

## Revenue targets → required volume

| Monthly revenue | GMV needed | Orders/mo | Orders/day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 AZN | 10,000 AZN | ~143 | ~5 |
| 10,000 AZN | 100,000 AZN | ~1,429 | ~48 |
| 50,000 AZN | 500,000 AZN | ~7,143 | ~238 |

> Reality check: at 10% / 70 AZN ticket, **50k/mo on commission alone is brutal**.
> To reach 50k you EITHER raise ticket (300–500 AZN niche work) OR add ad revenue
> (paid gig promotion) + PRO subscriptions. Don't plan 50k on 10% of 70 AZN.

## Lever analysis (which knob moves money fastest)
1. **AOV** — easiest early win. Bundle ("5 Reels/week" = 300 AZN) beats one-off 70 AZN.
2. **Repeat rate** — a client who orders weekly is worth 50× a one-off. Retention > acquisition.
3. **Take rate** — 10%→15% is +50% revenue with zero new users. Test it.
4. **Volume** — last, because it needs a working acquisition channel.

## The path that actually works to 10k/mo
- 200 active small-business clients
- ordering ~1 bundle/week (4 Reels = ~250 AZN)
- = 200 × 250 × 4 weeks = 200,000 GMV... too optimistic early.
- Realistic 10k: ~150 clients × 1 order/week × 70 AZN × 4 = 42,000 GMV = 4,200 AZN.
  → so you need BOTH more clients AND higher ticket. Push bundles from day 1.

## CAC ceiling (when you start paying for ads)
- LTV (rough) = AOV × commission × orders-per-client-lifetime.
  - e.g. 70 × 10% × 12 orders = 84 AZN LTV.
- **Max CAC must be < 1/3 LTV = ~28 AZN/client.** If a client costs more than that to
  acquire, paid ads lose money. Until then: free channels + manual outreach only.
