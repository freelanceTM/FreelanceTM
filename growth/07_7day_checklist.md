# 7-DAY EXECUTION — only money/users actions. Zero backend code.

Print this. Tick boxes. End each day filling the scoreboard.

## DAY 1 — Offer & economics
- [ ] Lock niche (default: Reels/TikTok editing for small biz)
- [ ] Fill `01_unit_economics.md` with YOUR ticket & commission
- [ ] Decide pricing: trial 30-50 AZN, weekly pack 4 Reels = 250 AZN
- [ ] Write your one-sentence offer

## DAY 2 — Build supply (editors)
- [ ] DM 20 editors (`02_supply_script.md`)
- [ ] Qualify, get portfolios
- [ ] Onboard 5-8 reliable ones, create their gig profiles
- [ ] Publish 3 showcase gigs

## DAY 3 — Marketing material
- [ ] Make 3 "before/after" or sample Reels (your own, as demos)
- [ ] Finalize `04_offer_landing.html` (your link to send)
- [ ] Prepare DM templates ready to paste

## DAY 4 — Demand outreach
- [ ] 30 Instagram DMs to weak-video businesses
- [ ] 5 in-person pitches (cafes/shops)
- [ ] Post offer in 5 local business/Telegram chats
- [ ] Log every contact in `05_deal_tracker.csv`

## DAY 5 — Close deal #1
- [ ] Convert a reply → trial order
- [ ] Dispatch to your best editor
- [ ] Collect payment (card/transfer to you)
- [ ] Deliver, take 10%. FIRST AZN. 🎉

## DAY 6 — Repeat x3-5
- [ ] More outreach (don't stop pipeline)
- [ ] Close 3-5 total deals this week
- [ ] After each trial, PITCH the weekly pack (retention = real money)

## DAY 7 — Money retro
- [ ] Count: deals, GMV, commission earned
- [ ] List every friction point that blocked a payment
- [ ] THAT list = your real backlog (not 'settlement retry')
